Hi, I'm Daniel 👋

I study biological nitrogen fixation and biogeochemical cycles in coastal upwelling systems, with a focus on the Rías Baixas (NW Spain). I work with the Biological Oceanography Group at CIM-UVIGO, combining fieldwork, incubation experiments and data analysis.
